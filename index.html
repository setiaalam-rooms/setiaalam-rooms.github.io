Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Can I see the place before deciding?

Yes. You can watch the video tours and photos on this page first, then book a viewing to see the place in person.

What comes with the room?

A bed with a brand-new mattress, study table, wardrobe, fan, air-conditioner, curtains and a water heater. New tenants also get a brand-new bed sheet set. Shared areas have a sofa, dining table and chairs, refrigerator, microwave, kettle and a pantry area for storage. Decorative items and props seen in the photos are for photography only and are not included.

What’s included in the rent?

WiFi is included. Air-con is metered per room, while water and the other electricity are shared by room. Message me for the details and current rates.

How does check-in work without keys?

The unit has a smart lock that opens with a passcode. I’ll set up your personal passcode before you arrive.

Can I use the pool and gym?

Yes. As a tenant of the unit, you can use the residence facilities — the infinity-edge pool, gym, playground, BBQ pit and more.

Are there any agent fees?

No. I’m the owner and I handle enquiries myself, so tenants don’t pay any agent fee. The tenancy agreement fee still applies — see the terms above.

Who can rent a room?

The unit is for Malaysian female tenants only, with one person per room. It is strictly non-smoking and no pets are allowed.

Can I have visitors or cook?

Day visitors are welcome in the common areas until 10pm, but no overnight guests please, as this is a female-only unit. Light cooking is fine — a microwave and kettle are provided. Please tidy up and ventilate after use.

Is this a female-only unit?

Yes. The whole unit is for female tenants only — ideal for working ladies and students.

Is there parking?

Yes, there is a covered car park, but only 1 bay is currently available to rent. Car park rent is RM 100 per month, plus an RM 150 access card.

What are the rent, deposit and minimum stay?

The minimum tenancy is 1 year, with 1.5 months’ advance rental and a 1.5-month utilities deposit — see the terms above. Message me for each room’s current rate, and please enquire to confirm the very latest details before you decide.
